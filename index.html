Goodu Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Goodu ("the app", "we", "us") is a group audio-calling app designed to let family households watch and talk together, including on a paired TV. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Microphone. The app uses your device's microphone to power real-time audio calls between the households you invite into a Gathering. Audio is transmitted directly (or via a relay, see below) to the other participants in your call and is not recorded or stored by us. Goodu does not use your camera.
  • Household and pairing identifiers. When you create or join a Gathering, the app generates a household identifier and a pairing code/password used to let your phone(s) and TV rejoin the same Gathering later. These identifiers are used only to route calls and playback between your own devices and the household(s) you choose to call; they are not used for advertising or shared with third parties.
  • Google Photos (optional). If you choose to share photos/videos during a Gathering, the app opens Google's own Photos Picker in your browser. You select exactly which items to share; the app never gets standing access to your full photo library, only the items you explicitly pick. This exchange is brokered through our backend server, which never stores your Google account password — Google, not us, authenticates you and hands back only the picked item references. To keep your shared media available if our server restarts, we store a Google access token for the Gathering on our server, encrypted at rest; it is deleted with the Gathering.

Diagnostics and crash reports

To find out why a call failed or the app crashed, Goodu sends diagnostic information to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), a third-party error-reporting service, and our own backend server writes similar logs. This covers: crash and error reports; connection details for a call (whether the microphone started, connection state, whether audio went direct or through the relay, packet counts and round-trip time, with no IP addresses); the type of network (Wi-Fi or cellular) and whether it was connected; whether the app was in the foreground or background; the app version, device model and operating system version; a random identifier generated by the app for your installation; and the Gathering and call identifiers needed to match two phones' reports of the same call. It does not include audio, photos, your contacts, or your name or email. We use it only to fix problems and do not use it for advertising or tracking.

If you tap "Send a report about this call" in Call details, a snapshot of that call's connection details is sent to us the same way. Sentry keeps this data for up to 30 days, and we can delete it on request.

How your call data is transmitted

Goodu calls use the WebRTC standard. Audio is sent peer-to-peer where possible. When a direct connection isn't possible (e.g. due to home network/NAT restrictions), the call is relayed through a third-party TURN relay service (Metered.ca) purely to forward encrypted media packets between participants — the relay does not store or inspect call content.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data or use it to track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising SDKs. The only third-party SDK that sends data about the app is Sentry, for crash and diagnostic reports as described above.
  • We do not record or store your calls.
  • We do not access your on-device photo library beyond the items you explicitly pick via Google's own Photos Picker.

Data retention

Gathering/household records (pairing codes, membership, queued media references) are kept on our backend server for as long as your household's Gathering remains active, so you can rejoin it later. You can ask us to delete this data at any time using the contact information below.

Children's privacy

Goodu is a family app intended to be used with a parent, guardian, or other adult family member setting up and supervising the Gathering. It is not directed at children using it independently, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without such supervision.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
