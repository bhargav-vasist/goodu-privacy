Goodu Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Goodu ("the app", "we", "us") is a group video-calling app designed to let family households watch and talk together, including on a paired TV. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Camera and microphone. The app uses your device's camera and microphone to power real-time video and audio calls between the households you invite into a Gathering. Camera/microphone streams are transmitted directly (or via a relay, see below) to the other participants in your call and are not recorded or stored by us.
  • Household and pairing identifiers. When you create or join a Gathering, the app generates a household identifier and a pairing code/password used to let your phone(s) and TV rejoin the same Gathering later. These identifiers are used only to route calls and playback between your own devices and the household(s) you choose to call; they are not used for advertising or shared with third parties.
  • Google Photos (optional). If you choose to share photos/videos during a Gathering, the app opens Google's own Photos Picker in your browser. You select exactly which items to share; the app never gets standing access to your full photo library, only the items you explicitly pick. This exchange is brokered through our backend server, which never stores your Google account credentials — Google, not us, authenticates you and hands back only the picked item references.

How your call data is transmitted

Goodu calls use the WebRTC standard. Audio/video is sent peer-to-peer where possible. When a direct connection isn't possible (e.g. due to home network/NAT restrictions), the call is relayed through a third-party TURN relay service (Metered.ca) purely to forward encrypted media packets between participants — the relay does not store or inspect call content.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs in the app.
  • We do not record or store your calls.
  • We do not access your on-device photo library beyond the items you explicitly pick via Google's own Photos Picker.

Data retention

Gathering/household records (pairing codes, membership, queued media references) are kept on our backend server for as long as your household's Gathering remains active, so you can rejoin it later. You can ask us to delete this data at any time using the contact information below.

Children's privacy

Goodu is a family app intended to be used with a parent, guardian, or other adult family member setting up and supervising the Gathering. It is not directed at children using it independently, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without such supervision.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.
